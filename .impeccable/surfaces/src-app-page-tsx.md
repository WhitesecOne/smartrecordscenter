# Surface brief: public website (Bahasa Indonesia), corporate rebuild 2026-10-06

Scope: every public route under `src/app` (Beranda; Modul index + 7 module pages; Platform x4; Industri; Mengapa Kami; Tentang Kami; Kontak; Terima Kasih; legal; 404). Mode: Persuade. Light theme (owner decision).

Audience: records managers, compliance/legal, CIOs at regulated Indonesian organizations (pemerintah, BUMN/BUMD, perbankan, korporasi). Action: Minta Demo (contact form). Proof: real photography of archives and institutions (Unsplash License, provenance in sidecars), coded product screens labelled "Data contoh", an in-browser SHA-256 audit-chain check. No invented customers, stats, testimonials or certifications.

Information architecture (user, 2026-10-06): seven modules replace the old five, all named in Bahasa Indonesia, each with its own AI capability: Repositori Arsip, Pencarian & Temu Kembali, Manajemen Metadata, Klasifikasi Arsip, Retensi & Penyusutan, Jejak Audit, Pelaporan. Header: Modul, Platform, Industri, Perusahaan, Kontak, Minta Demo.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A formal Indonesian corporate site for records governance where real photography of archives, institutions and people at work sets the authority and real product screens prove each of the seven modules. Refuses the dark neon AI hero, icon-card mosaics, gradient text and invented proof.
OWN-WORLD: Navy #0B2447 owns whole regions: the photographic hero veil, the security band, the CTA band, the footer. White and cool mist #F4F6FA carry reading. Corporate blue #1D4ED8 is the only action colour. Red Hat Display headings (650 to 700, tight), Red Hat Text body, Red Hat Mono only for record codes and hashes. 8px controls, 12px cards and frames, hairline borders, one soft navy shadow for product frames and menus. Status colours live only inside product frames.
STORY: The visitor sees the archive and the promise, scans the seven modules and each module's own screen, follows one record from capture to disposition, sees AI kept under human review, verifies the audit chain themselves, finds their sector, and books a demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed archive-shelf photograph under a left-weighted navy veil, about 88vh. Left half: h1 at display scale in white, lead at 80% white, blue "Minta Demo" and white-outline "Lihat Tujuh Modul", one line of regulatory references. A white module index bar overlaps the photo's bottom edge and lists the seven modules as links. Signature interaction: the home module explorer, where choosing a module swaps its product screen.
FORM: canon executed at full craft, pinned by the user in words ("gaya corporate", "korporat berbasis foto"); craft bar: IBM, Microsoft Purview, OpenText. Seed key: none (user-pinned direction, concept-seed not run).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
